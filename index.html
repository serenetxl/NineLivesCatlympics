The Stories That Defined Catlympics

💀
The Finals Bloodbath
The most devastating match of the event. 22 bets, 72,455 CC wagered — and only 2 people got it right. The correct answer was 1:Feilong · 2:Junhao · 3:Yangkai. Twenty people got it wrong. Combined losses: 69,773 CC. xZel lost 15,476 in one bet. Chion lost 10,000. Tsukiyue 9,231. Serene 8,718. The ×20 multiplier giveth, and the ×20 multiplier taketh away.
🪑
Sky Survival: Everyone Was Right
The polar opposite of the Finals. Round 2 (Sky Survival) had 27 bets placed — and all 27 won. A 100% win rate. The guild collectively backed Feilong, Junhao, and Yangkai to survive and got it right every single time. The most harmonious match of the entire event.
🐱
Mici's 1,500 CC That Changed Everything
Mici bet just 1,500 CC on the correct Finals ranking at ×20 odds. That's a net profit of +30,000 CC — the single biggest payout of the event by a massive margin. For context: D-Chikaru bet only 142 CC on the same pick and still walked away with +2,840 CC. Two players read the room perfectly while twenty others didn't.
🎯
D-Chikaru: The Perfect Record
Seven bets. Seven wins. 7W / 0L — the only player to go undefeated across the entire event. Net profit of +6,178 CC. D-Chikaru picked correctly in every single round without ever getting it wrong once. Quietly, efficiently, flawlessly.
📈
Huangji: The Almost
Went 6W/8L overall but hit the biggest mid-event peak of any non-Finals winner — +11,243 CC at one point. A 7,000 CC Finals-2 bet for +9,310 CC had them looking like the star of the event. Then the Finals happened. Three wrong bets, −10,000 CC, and a final net of just +1,243 CC. So close.
🔁
Feilong: Picked by Everyone, Paid by Nobody
Feilong appeared in nearly every bet across every round — as a contestant, they were the consensus favourite all event long. Almost every winning pick included Feilong's name. Yet in the Finals, getting Feilong right wasn't enough — you needed the exact order too. Most people had Feilong at #1, but got #2 and #3 wrong. That's what made the Finals brutal.
